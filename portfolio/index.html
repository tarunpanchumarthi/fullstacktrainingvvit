About

I'm an AIML undergraduate at VVIT with a habit of turning coursework into working software — a records app, a health app, a prediction model — rather than leaving it on paper.

My interest sits at the point where data meets an interface someone can actually use: Python and machine learning on one side, Java, Flask and Flutter on the other. I like problems with a visible outcome — a dashboard someone checks, an app someone opens — and I'm looking for a team where I can keep building real things and pick up the practices that only come from shipping in production.
